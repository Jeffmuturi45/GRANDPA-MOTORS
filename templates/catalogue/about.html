Who We Are

Nairobi's Dealership
You Can Rely On

Grandpa Motors was founded with a single purpose: to make buying a vehicle in Kenya a straightforward, honest, and dependable experience.

The Kenyan used-vehicle market has long been complicated by incomplete documentation, undisclosed mileage, and opaque pricing. We set out to change that. Every vehicle we list has been reviewed by our team, and we publish the full documentation status — logbook, duty, import documents, and transfer readiness — before a customer makes first contact.

We believe that transparency is not a selling point. It is a baseline. Our role is to connect buyers with vehicles they can trust, and to stand behind that connection.
